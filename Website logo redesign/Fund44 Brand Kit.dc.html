04 · Clear space & minimum size

fund44

Clear space on all four sides equals the height of the lowercase d — about 0.6× the plate height. Nothing enters it: no nav links, no rules, no photo edges.

fund44 fund44

Minimum 16px cap height on screen, 0.4in in print. Border is 1px up to 24px and 2px above — otherwise the plate looks hairline at large sizes. Below 16px, use the square icon instead.

06 · Construction

TypefaceFigtree ExtraBold (800) — Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License, free for commercial use and web embedding.
Case & trackingAll lowercase. Letter-spacing −0.045em on "fund", −0.05em on "44".
Digits0.86em of the wordmark size so the 44 sits at lowercase height, filled lime with a 0.04em ink outline drawn behind the fill.
PlateWhite, 1px–2px ink border, radius 0.34em, padding 0.26em vertical / 0.4em horizontal. All em-based, so it scales from 16px to a billboard.
